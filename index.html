ABOUT

SAT QUEST is a satellite mission-design challenge by Parikrama, the Satellite Club of NIT Rourkela, made for Innovision 2026, the tech fest of the Technical Society, Student Activity Centre.

NIT Rourkela's Student Satellite Club. Exploring the cosmos.

Parikrama (परिक्रमा) means “going around”, which is exactly what a satellite does. We are a student community exploring satellites, orbital mechanics and space technology.
